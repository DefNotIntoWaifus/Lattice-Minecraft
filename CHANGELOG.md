# What's changed

## 0.2.0

The in-game menus are new, and a lot more of the game works.

**Menus**
- New look for the inventory, trading and every other menu: pale stone with dark carved slots, all
  on one pixel grid so item pictures stay sharp at any window size.
- The world dims properly behind a menu, and chat and the hotbar get out of the way.
- Your character wears the armour you have on, and active effects are listed beside the inventory.
- Trading: lists longer than the window now scroll (before, some trades couldn't be reached at all),
  prices show today's real cost including discounts and demand, sold-out trades are marked, the
  chosen trade shows in its slots, and tooltips work on every item in the list.
- Picking up and dragging items now works like the game: pressing picks up, dragging spreads with a
  live preview, and double-click and shift-double-click gather stacks.
- The beacon now asks you to choose powers and confirm with Done, instead of taking the first click.
- The keys for dropping and swapping in menus follow your key bindings.

**New things you can use**
- Command blocks, including in minecarts, with tab completion. Structure blocks, jigsaw blocks,
  test blocks and test instance blocks.
- Game rules and difficulty, from the pause menu, for players allowed to change them.
- Game mode switcher on F3 and F4, and more F3 shortcuts: B hit boxes, G chunk borders, H item
  details, N spectator, C copy position, I copy block or mob data, D clear chat, T reload packs,
  A redraw the world.
- Crafter slots switch on and off, and the crafter shows when it's powered.
- Bundles show what's in them, and the wheel chooses what comes out.
- Saved hotbars in creative: C and a number saves, X and a number loads.
- A Leave Bed button. Before, there was no way out of bed until morning.
- Leaving the End shows the closing story and takes you home. Before, you got stuck.
- Totems of Undying show their effect and play their sound.
- Compass needles point to spawn, lodestones or your last death, and spin where they have nothing
  to point at.

**Fixes**
- The crafter's inventory was one slot out, so items went somewhere other than where you clicked.
- Typing the letter of the inventory key in an anvil or search box closed the menu.
- Copper chests and other blocks no longer try to place the block you're holding when you use them.
- Servers can take back chat messages.
- Scoreboard, respawn and low-disk messages from the server that were being dropped are now shown.

**Releases**
- Lattice now has versions and releases. The launcher tells you when there's a newer one and can
  install it for you. Turn that off under Settings, Interface.
